(roadmap) (how ROADMAP.md and its detail docs are written):
* `docs/ROADMAP.md` is the list: under every `##` / `###` heading one `> ` summary line (at most 160 characters), then one line per item. `roadmap/<section>/<slug>.md` holds one item's facts; `roadmap/<section>/README.md` holds the section's notes, rules and State. A README holds each old prose line it keeps as `* ` bullets cut where the cut rule (below) cuts, side by side at the line's depth, each cut leaving its `;` or `.` on the piece before it, so that the pieces joined by a space are the line as kept.
* section directories are the ROADMAP section numbers as written: `0`, `0-B`, `0-C`, `1` (notes only), `2-N`, `2-R`, `2`, `3` (notes only), `3.a`, `3.b`, `3.c`, `4`, `5` (notes only), `5.b`, `5.2`, `6`, `7`, `8`.
* a table row splits into cells at ` | `, never inside a code span (a code span may hold `|` or an unpaired backtick); a §5.2 queue row's item cell is the rest after two cells from the left and before two from the right, a §7 row's item cell is its second cell.
* list line, every section but the tables: `- [① ]<id> — [<title>](roadmap/<section>/<slug>.md)[ · <STATUS>[ (part)]]`; `①` marks a CRITICAL silent-wrong as the old line did; `<STATUS>` is the line's status word (the last one outside its parentheticals); ` (part)` follows it when its ref gives that status to part of the item only. The ref itself is the doc's `status:` bullet, verbatim.
* list row, §5.2: `| <id> | <order> · <track>[ (<states>)] | [<title>](roadmap/5.2/<slug>.md) | <rank> |`; `<rank>` is the rank only (`①`, `② F`, `— C`), anything after it is a `note:` bullet in the doc; `<order> · <track>` is the slot head verbatim; `<states>` is the states the slot gives this row (`PARKED`, `held`, `delivered`, `landed`, `BLOCKED`, …), as the slot spells them, in slot order, deduplicated, each followed by ` (part)` when the slot gives it to part of the item only; a row over 200 characters shortens its title, never the slot head.
* §5.2 state words (2026-11-17, roadmap review G9; each as the slot spells it): `landed` the row's code is on main, its commit named in the doc; `built` its code is on an integration branch, not on main; `PARKED` (older spelling `parked`) an attempt stopped with nothing landed and the doc names its re-entry; `un-parked` a park lifted, the row startable at its slot; `held` not started on purpose (an owner decision or a trigger), the doc names what releases it; `delivered` a no-code row whose census or design exists; ` (part)` the state covers part of the item.
* §5.2 tracks: `L1`, `L2`, `T3` and `any` as roadmap/5.2/README.md defines them (its Since 2026-10-28 line); `held` a row in no run order until its trigger fires; `hygiene` row 10's review-free track, at most one item beside a row (roadmap/5.2/README.md's first line).
* slot and status refs: the slot's whole parenthetical, verbatim and byte for byte, is the doc's `slot:` bullets, split only at its top-level `; `; a status ref, verbatim, is the doc's `status:` bullet.
* list row, §7: the item cell is `[<title>](roadmap/7/<slug>.md)`. The trigger stays in its column verbatim; when the row would pass 200 characters, the column keeps the trigger without its trailing top-level parenthetical, and the doc's `re-entry:` bullet holds the whole trigger verbatim.
* kept as they are, no doc: the §5.a verdict table, the §6 stage table, note lines of at most 80 characters, and §5.2's Do-not-start lines (`- <ref> — <reason>`); a kept line past 200 characters is cut: a short line (a row keeps its cell count and first cell, a `- ` line its status word) whose end, or a row's last cell, is ` ([note](roadmap/<section>/README.md))`, the old line whole as a `note:` bullet in that README.
* caps: a title at most 80 characters, with no status word; every line of `docs/ROADMAP.md` at most 200 characters, no exception.
* detail doc: line 1 is `(<Info>) (<title>):`, the title the same as the list line's. Then one `* ` bullet per clause of the item's text (the cut rule below) and per `slot:`, `status:`, `source:`, `note:` or `re-entry:` piece, in the old line's order, at the top level, but a clause of an indented sub-line of the old line is a `  * ` bullet, as the old line indents it. A bullet starts with one label or with none; the labels are `scope:`, `symptom:`, `witness:`, `oracles:`, `cause:`, `fix:`, `evidence:`, `pins:`, `status:`, `slot:`, `re-entry:`, `record:`, `note:`, `source:`.
* cut rule: an item's text is cut at every sentence end (a `. ` whose `.` does not follow another `.`) and at every `; `, each outside code spans, `"` quotes and parentheticals, but not after the first ` · ` outside them in its cell, line or sub-line; it is cut nowhere else. Each piece is one bullet, whole whatever its length, its parentheticals inline: the `.` stays at its end, the `; ` is dropped, a leading `① ` is dropped (the list line carries it), and a label word the old text starts it with stays, as its label. An item's text is a table row's item cell, or a list line after its id up to a trailing `STATUS (…)` ref (the `status:` bullet), and each of its indented sub-lines, each cut on its own.
* `<Info>`, a row with a rank (§5.2, §7): `§<section> <id> · rank <rank> · source <source>`, `<source>` the whole source when it fits in 80 characters, else the source up to its last top-level `, ` / `; ` within 80 characters (never inside `( )`), the rest a `source:` bullet; with no such cut the whole source is the `source:` bullet and the Info has no `· source`. Other items: `§<section> <id>`, then ` · <STATUS>` when the list line has a status word, then ` · in <group>` when the old line stood under a `###` subsection or a group label. The status and the rank equal the list line's (the list line is the master; the check fails on a difference); the slot head and its state tokens are in the list, the slot's clauses in `slot:` bullets.
* slug: the id with `🆕 ` → `new-`, a circled digit → its number, a circled letter → its letter, any other character outside `A-Za-z0-9._-` → `-`; unique within its directory ignoring case.
* ids are stable and never reused: a new item takes a new id (§5.2 `Q-<NAME>`; §2 the next 🆕 letter; §3.a the next circled letter of its number; elsewhere a kebab slug of at most four words), never one that a commit, a source comment or a W record has used.
* filing: a new item is one list line plus one doc; a filing onto an existing item (a witness, a cause, a park reason) is bullets in its doc, witness cells and oracle text whole there and only there; the list line changes only its status, slot or rank.
* Grounding re-measures every claim (LOOPROMPT §1), so a line keeps only what a re-measurement cannot recover: the id, the code site, a held design's branch or commit, a named prerequisite.
* done: delete the list line and the doc in one commit.
* kept from the old ROADMAP header, verbatim:
  * A finished slice's record is its commit message, anchored `§4.5.N` (bug, mechanism, byte-identity, review): find it with `git log --grep '§4.5.N'`;
  * entries up to §4.5.582 are in `git show acabe991:docs/history/ROADMAP_ARCHIVE.md`.
  * Section numbers and row ids are stable, never renumbered or reused;
  * source comments cite them (`ROADMAP §2 🆕 I ⓐ`, `§3 ⑭`, `§3.b <slug>`, `§5.1-be`).
  * No gate or test counts in any doc: CI on 3 OSes is the record, and `format_version` is `crates/vita-artifact/src/header.rs:15`.
  * STATUS: OPEN = startable (two oracles, or one plus hand-IEEE, and no unmet prerequisite).
  * Nothing else starts: BLOCKED (a named prerequisite, listed in [REMAINING_WORK.md](../REMAINING_WORK.md) §D), WALL, SPLIT (oracle split, never chased), HELD (deliberate or trigger-gated), RECORD (observation), LOUD, PERF, DEFERRED, DO-NOT-START, DUP.
* rewritten from the old header: its format sentence (`id — symptom (headline cell); code site; fix shape; oracles; STATUS`) is the list-line and doc grammar above; its done sentence (`Done = delete the closed line(s), add each new find as one line in its section, recount the Summary.`) is the done and filing bullets above.
* the Direction block (roadmap migration step S1, 2026-11-17): `## Direction` opens `docs/ROADMAP.md`, before §0, with six `###` parts in this order: The end (where the program ends, and the words opens and runs), Scoreboard (one dated line: originals running / 23, twin-only, twin fails), Goal ladder (one line per goal), State (the one statement of now), Order rules (precedence P1–P5, rules O1–O9), Current chain (the current goal's rows in run order, then the next goal's); it holds no count table, no slot number, no journal and nothing a row measured, and its lines have no doc.
* goal ladder line: the list-line grammar above, with the goal id as id, what runs as title, `roadmap/goals/<G-ID>.md` as link, then ` · <state> · <n> / 23`, `<state>` one of `reached`, `current`, `next`, `later`, `proposed`, and `<n>` the scoreboard's originals running once the goal is reached; the goal doc's line 1 carries the same title.
* chain table: `| # | id | title | state |` in the Direction block (a goal doc's chain adds its kind and why, goals/README.md), one row per chain position in run order, the title the row's list title verbatim and unlinked (the list line links it), a row split by ` (part)` written as two positions with its cut beside the id (`24 (EH1 cut)`, `24 (OT cut)`, `29 (page half)`), the state one of the six state words below.
* directories beside the section directories: `goals/` holds one doc per goal, its grammar in [goals/README.md](goals/README.md); `history/` holds what leaves a live file, verbatim, one file per move named `<what>-<date>.md`, each linked once from `docs/ROADMAP.md` where its text stood, its line 1 the `(<Info>) (<title>):` head and its moved text below, in a code block where the text's links pointed from another directory.
* row-doc head block (written by migration steps S4 and S5, and by every new §5.2 row from then on): directly under line 1, five labelled bullets, `* purpose:` (the form or record, one sentence, its IEEE clause), `* why:` (the goal and designs it is on the path of, or `no goal — K<n> ; trigger: <the group's trigger line>`), `* needs:` (row ids), `* readers:` (row ids) and `* state:` (one of the six state words, dated), then the marker bullet `* record: as filed below ; superseded order lines are kept for the record`.
* below the marker stands the record as filed: witnesses with oracle text (one per distinct symptom is the item's identity and stays), park lines, re-entry lines and pointers to the slice's own records under W; a new line there is never a cell table, a cell count, a gate or test count, a line number, a round-by-round finding or a superseded order line (those belong to the slice's records and the commit message); what already stands below the marker stays as it is.
* the six state words (forward plan 4.2; owner Q6 (a), 2026-11-17), for head blocks, goal docs and chain tables: `OPEN` startable, its needs met; `WAITS <id>` a named row not yet landed; `PARKED <id>` tried, and it returns when `<id>` lands; `DORMANT` parked with no route (ROADMAP.md's Order rules, O4); `BUILT` on the integration branch, not on main; `LANDED` on main.
* mapping from the §5.2 table's words, applied by migration step S2: `PARKED` or `parked` → `PARKED <id>` or `DORMANT` by whether a buildable prerequisite is named; `held` → `WAITS <id>` or `DORMANT` likewise; `planned` → `OPEN` or `WAITS <id>`; `built` → `BUILT`; `landed` and `delivered` → `LANDED`; `un-parked` → `OPEN`; ` (part)` → the row is split in the chain table (24 EH1 cut / 24 OT cut; 29 page half / 29 full form).
* until step S2 the §5.2 table keeps its slot column and its state words and tracks as the two bullets above define them; the slot column and the tracks then retire to `history/` (owner Q6 (a), 2026-11-17); the rank column stays, a class, not an order.
* update rule (forward plan 4.3; who changes what, one line each):
  * row start (main): read the Direction block, the goal doc and the row doc; if the row doc's head block disagrees with the goal doc, fix the doc before the brief.
  * row built (the row's docs step): the row doc's `state: BUILT`; the goal doc's chain state; the State line.
  * landing (the landing docs commit, by the clerk after main reads the diff): the rows to the Landed list with their §4.5.N (from step S2; until then the table row is marked landed), the doc staying where it is (code head notes cite it); the goal doc's `batches`; the State line; the scoreboard if a design now runs.
  * landing, the checklist: the landing docs commit re-reads the lines of `docs/IEEE_CHECKLIST.md` (the whole language, clause by clause, as check boxes) for the clauses its rows touch.
  * park (the park docs commit): the row doc's `state` plus one park line (date, class, pointer) and its re-entry; the goal doc: the prerequisite row inserted before it, or DORMANT and an O6 note; the State line.
  * owner decision (main, the same day): the State line if "now" changes; the ladder if the order changes; the goal doc's `decisions` line; a rule's text if a rule changes; HANDOFF gets a pointer, not a copy.
  * new finding (lens or docs agent): one entry in its section, or bullets on its existing entry; never in the Direction block or a goal doc.
  * goal reached (main): the ladder's state, the scoreboard, the next goal's doc re-read (checkpoint C3, goals/README.md).
  * a new row id: one list line plus one doc with a head block; if it is on a chain, the goal doc's chain in the same commit.
* citations, for every new line: name the fn and the file, never a line number; a doc cites another doc by `<slug>.md` and a label, never by `:N`.
